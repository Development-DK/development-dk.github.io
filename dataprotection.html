Privacy Policy

This app (Salmon PDF) does not collect or store any personal data from users. No data is sent to the developer.

Use of Apple Services

The app only uses Apple technologies:

  • StoreKit for in-app purchases. Only the information Apple needs to process a purchase is handled.
  • iCloud Drive, if you store your documents there. They are kept in your own iCloud account.

Apple Intelligence

Summaries and the AI chat use Apple Intelligence. By default, everything is processed on your device and your documents never leave it.

In the chat, you can optionally choose Private Cloud Compute as the model. Your questions and the text of the document are then sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute servers to create the answer. According to Apple, this data is only used to answer your request, is not stored, and cannot be accessed by Apple. Summaries are always created on your device. You can switch back to the on-device model at any time.

Data Sharing

Apart from the Apple services described above, no data is shared with third parties, and no information is used for analytics or advertising purposes.
